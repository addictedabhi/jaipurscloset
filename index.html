Rooted in Rajasthan

We are a family-run boutique bringing the living craft traditions of Jaipur straight to you. Every piece in our collection — from hand-block printed cotton sarees to vivid bandhani dupattas and mirror-work kurtas — is sourced directly from Rajasthani artisans who have perfected their craft across generations.

Our mission is simple: authentic ethnic wear at honest prices, with the warmth of a personal shopping experience. Browse our collections here and order directly through Instagram DM — we are always happy to help you find the perfect outfit for any occasion.
